# Initial HDIP Repository Baseline

This initial commit creates the independent home for **HDIP — Holistic Data, Information and Intelligence Platform**.

## Imported from the BPS-era HDIP material

Only reconciled HDIP content is migrated in this baseline:

1. `hdip-brief.md`
2. `hdip-data-product-development-stages.md`
3. `hdip-enterprise-level1-data-architecture-requirements.md`
4. `hdip-product-consumption-stages.md`
5. `hdip-product-descriptor.md`
6. `hdip-synctx-ontology.md`
7. `hdip-synctx-pdep-inference.md`
8. `hdip-synthetic-context.md`

The images directly referenced by those documents are also included.

## Deliberately not migrated yet

The initial baseline intentionally excludes BPS-era HDIP material that has not yet been reconciled for the independent HDIP architecture, including:

- `hdip-enterprise-level2-data-architecture-requirements.md`;
- legacy HDIP interactive flow-player pages/components;
- other BPS/UPOS/Product-specification content that is authoritative in BPS rather than HDIP.

Those assets can be reviewed and migrated later if they remain valid.
