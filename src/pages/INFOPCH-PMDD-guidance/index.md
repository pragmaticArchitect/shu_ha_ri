# Human and AI Agent guidance

> **Experimental preparation, not an operative maturity engine.** The earlier P03 reservation remains a programme/release boundary. Experimental IPCH01 guidance exists under IPCH P01 decisions D01–D10, but its G4–G6 reviews have not been executed and no normative adoption is implied.

Use the **[PMDD IPCH Maturity Guidance catalogue](/docs/pmdd/ipch-maturity-guidance)** for the authoritative *research-draft location* of individual characteristic criteria. The first available guide is **[IPCH01 — Domain-Owned](/docs/infopch/IPCH01)** (`DRAFT-02`, experimental). See also [PMDD assessment guidance](/docs/pmdd/assessment-guidance) for the shared human, deterministic and agent-assisted approach.

An assessor, whether human or automated, should:

1. Verify independent assessment eligibility for the exact Product and Version.
2. Load the individual characteristic guidance and any documented enterprise assessment profile.
3. Examine all mandatory characteristic claims against competent establishing, supporting and contrary evidence.
4. Preserve material contradictions and missing evidence rather than converting uncertainty into an unsupported maturity result.
5. Determine a reasoned characteristic-level finding, then use the separate experimental PMDD scoring method where valid.
6. Preserve evidence traceability, authority boundaries and competent owner review.

**Division of responsibility:** PMDD characteristic documents define *what adherence means*; the enterprise profile defines *how much evidence and assurance is required* without diluting mandatory claims; the agent or human workflow determines *how evidence is collected and examined*. Agent output does not create Productness, semantic authority, policy permission or accountable governance.

The IPCH01 guide remains a prepared experimental draft. G4 adversarial validation, G5 final guide disposition, G6 catalogue reconciliation and any normative release remain outstanding.
