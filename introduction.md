# HDIP — Holistic Data, Information and Intelligence Platform

**HDIP** is an enterprise logical platform architecture for governed, interoperable and product-oriented capabilities across **Data, Information and Intelligence**.

Its architectural purpose is to compose and realize responsibilities from the relevant generic reference architectures while preserving the semantic and Product authorities that define the meaning of the things HDIP operates on.

## Canonical proposition

> **HDIP is an enterprise logical platform architecture that provides governed, interoperable and product-oriented capabilities across Data, Information and Intelligence by composing and realizing responsibilities defined by GEDRA, GEARA and GEAIRA, while preserving the semantic and Product authorities of GEDRM, GEARM, GEAIRM, BPS/PIN and applicable Product-family specifications.**

Short form:

> **GEDRA, GEARA and GEAIRA define generic architectural responsibilities; HDIP composes and realizes those responsibilities as one enterprise platform.**

## HDIP is not a fourth generic reference architecture

```text
GEDRA / GEARA / GEAIRA
        |
        v
     HDIP-CLA
 enterprise platform
 composition/realization
        |
        v
technology realization
```

HDIP is therefore a realization and composition architecture rather than a peer semantic or generic reference model.

## Product is an orthogonal lens

BPS/PIN remains the authority for generic Product semantics. HDIP development, deployment, registration, cataloguing, composition or consumption does not by itself establish Product identity.

## Repository baseline

This initial repository imports the reconciled HDIP material formerly maintained under BPS and provides the home for the **HDIP Canonical Logical Architecture programme — HDIP-CLA 0.1**.
