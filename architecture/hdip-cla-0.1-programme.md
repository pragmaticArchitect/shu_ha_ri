# HDIP Canonical Logical Architecture — HDIP-CLA 0.1

This page records the programme structure for deriving the canonical logical architecture of the **Holistic Data, Information and Intelligence Platform (HDIP)**.

The programme is responsibility-first and deliberately avoids freezing logical planes, overlays or Architecture Building Blocks before the upstream responsibilities and existing HDIP constructs have been systematically analysed.

## Foundational proposition

> **HDIP is an enterprise logical platform architecture that provides governed, interoperable and product-oriented capabilities across Data, Information and Intelligence by composing and realizing responsibilities defined by GEDRA, GEARA and GEAIRA, while preserving the semantic and Product authorities of GEDRM, GEARM, GEAIRM, BPS/PIN and applicable Product-family specifications.**

## Programme guardrails

- HDIP is an enterprise logical platform realization/composition architecture, not a fourth peer generic reference architecture.
- Product semantics remain authoritative in BPS/PIN and applicable Product-family specifications.
- HDIP is not synonymous with Data Mesh; Data Mesh may influence Data-domain realization.
- HDIP-CLA remains technology-neutral.
- Canonical planes and ABBs are derived from responsibilities rather than inherited from historical diagrams.
- Shared realization must not collapse semantic distinctions.
- HDIP-generated descriptors, projections and compiled artifacts do not become semantic authorities.
- Lifecycle, deployment, registration, cataloguing or consumption does not itself establish Productness.
- Historical meaning of versioned HDIP artifacts must be preserved.
- Every canonical plane, overlay and ABB must be traceable to an upstream reference-architecture responsibility, explicit enterprise requirement, or justified HDIP-specific coordination responsibility.

## Initial repository state

The repository begins with reconciled HDIP concept and lifecycle material migrated from BPS. Formal HDIP-CLA stage outputs will be added incrementally as the programme proceeds.
