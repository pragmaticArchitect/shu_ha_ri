# INFOPS-P01-TB-B01-F01 — Research Charter, Scope and Falsifiability Boundary

**Owner disposition:** `APPROVE`, 8 November 2020. **Approved:** `F01.1–F01.20`, without amendment. This is an owner-approved, **non-normative** research charter. Repository adoption of this B01 record has not yet been established; completion of B01 and authorization of B02–B06 are not established.

**Principal research question:** Does an Information Product possess intrinsic, discriminating Product-family characteristics independent of implementation, realization, organizational ownership and consumption mechanisms?

## Competing hypotheses

- **H1:** Distinct intrinsic Information Product family.
- **H2:** Derived, specialized or composite Product classification.
- **H3:** Relational/contextual classification rather than intrinsic Product Kind.
- **H4:** Alternative ontology required because Q0–Q5 is inadequate.

These are not presumed true, mutually exclusive or exhaustive. Negative and inconclusive findings are valid outcomes.

## Approved decision clauses

### F01.1 — Purpose

Investigate intrinsic Information Product-family discrimination; prioritize discovery and falsification over confirmation.

### F01.2 — Prior research

Inherit prospective conditional S04 closure; retain historical C06 approved-not-effective and four 65/6/7/7 ledgers plus separate PC decisions.

### F01.3 — Principal question

Determine whether Information Product has a defensible intrinsic constitution distinct from other Product families, independent of implementation and consumption.

### F01.4 — Competing hypotheses

H1 intrinsic family, H2 derived/composite classification, H3 relational/contextual classification, H4 alternative ontology; no presumed truth or exhaustiveness.

### F01.5 — Candidate Q0-Q5

Q0 independent BPS/PIN Productness; Q1-Q4 universal necessity unestablished; Q0-Q4 joint sufficiency unestablished, not formally disproven; Q5 separate procedural adjudication.

### F01.6 — Falsifiability

Define subject/Version, claim, counterexample, admissible evidence, oracle, outcome, uncertainty; distinguish conceptual from empirical methods.

### F01.7 — Original falsifications

Preserve F06/F14/F16 unresolved with original evidence and assumptions; no retrospective execution or closure.

### F01.8 — Family boundaries

Investigate C02/C21 enriched Data vs Information, C12/C18/C31 multi-Promise and Compound, AI dependency, context, semantics and realization.

### F01.9 — Identity invariants

Separate Product Kind/instance/Version, Information Proposition, semantic-definition/Version, realization revision, provider/contract Version, plan/binding/compilation, occurrence, evidence, determination, applicability/currentness.

### F01.10 — Independent authority

Preserve BPS/PIN, GEARM/GEARA, independent Data/AI/Service/Creative/Compound, BPS DPP Core, UPOS/PFI/HDIP, policy/legal and A1-A9 authorities.

### F01.11 — Evidence discipline

### F01.12 — Experiment authorization

### F01.13 — Adversarial classification

Distinguish supporting, contradictory, inconclusive, invalid and out-of-scope evidence; no generalization beyond claim.

### F01.14 — Negative findings

Record alternative explanations, negative results and problem reframing; no mandated conclusion that an intrinsic Information family exists.

### F01.15 — Reproducibility
