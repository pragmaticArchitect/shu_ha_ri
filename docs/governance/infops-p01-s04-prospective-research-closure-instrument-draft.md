## 1. Purpose, authority and non-purpose

This instrument proposes **new, explicitly prospective conditions** by which the accountable KivaNura.org owner could later assess whether the bounded P01-S04 exploratory research stage is suitable for the classification `CLOSED_CONDITIONAL_RESEARCH (ONTOLOGY_AND_NORMATIVE_READINESS_UNRESOLVED)` or another classification expressly chosen in a later competent decision. It does not make the previously approved S04-C06 target effective. It does not determine that INFOPS is ready for normative publication or that an intrinsically Information Product has been fully discriminated from Data, AI/Intelligence, Service, Creative or Compound Product families.

`INFOPS-GOV-MANDATE-0.1` prospectively authorizes the human owner to formulate, adopt and evaluate *internal research-stage* criteria, subject to actual independent legal, policy and upstream authority constraints. The owner-attested Git adoption of that mandate is not asserted here as an independently audited commit. The design record `TA01-F06` authorizes **preparation of this draft only**, not the adoption of any PC criterion or a stage-status transition.

This instrument introduces no standing committee, external signatory or mandatory certification ceremony. It requires an explicit owner disposition and traceable evidence appropriate to INFOPS 0.1. Independence is a property of the *separate assessment and decision steps*, not a claim that a second legal approver exists.

## 2. Temporal effect and adoption sequence

The following steps have **distinct authority effects**:

1. **Prepare** this draft using `TA01-F06` design approval. Current step; no PC criterion is binding.
2. **Review and explicitly approve/amend/reject/defer** the proposed instrument through a future, individually identified owner decision. Approval of the instrument is distinct from owner approval of `TA01-F06` design.
3. **Record a governing instrument** in the designated Git branch under the owner's lightweight governance controls. Record actual commit SHA and effective date from that event; an unapproved draft commit does not, by itself, constitute owner adoption. If the separately approved instrument specifies a later date, use that later date.
4. **Assess adopted criteria** against admissible evidence in a separately recorded criterion-by-criterion evaluation. Do not report pre-adoption material as an evaluation against binding criteria.
5. **Make a further, distinct accountable closure-effectiveness decision** with explicit reasoning, residual risks, temporal effect and repository traceability, if the adopted requirements permit it. Until such a decision validly takes effect, `S04 = NOT_CLOSED`.

No step backdates the criteria, changes earlier owners' decisions, or asserts that the unknown original S04 closure prerequisites were historically satisfied or invalid.

## 3. Three categories and interpretation

The proposed criteria are organized as:

- **Instrument validity (`PC-01` to `PC-03`):** confirm competence, prospective scope and a reasoned relationship with the prior C06 safeguards.
- **Bounded research-stage acceptance (`PC-04` to `PC-09`):** assess documented research completion *within explicit limitations*, without importing normative release standards.
- **Closure effectiveness (`PC-10`):** require a later separate accountable decision. PC-10 is a **decision control**, not a logically prior research-evidence result; its fulfillment cannot be claimed while that decision has not occurred.

The grouping is specific to S04 research governance. It is **not** a replacement for A1-A9 independent Product, Family, semantic, realization, Passport, fitness, maturity, consumption or normative-source determinations.

### 4.1 Acceptance is about *bounded completion*, not the absence of open research

For PC-07, PC-08 and PC-09, `SATISFIED` (if a future evaluation supports that result) would mean **the required limitations and obligations were accurately preserved and evidence-backed**, not that ontology questions were solved, empirical designs executed or release holds closed.

For PC-06, the existence of unverified original authority is explicitly allowed to remain an *observed limitation*; a `SATISFIED` result would instead require a reasoned and competent decision on the implications for a **separate prospective basis**. This is not proof that any unknown original requirement was satisfied. If a known mandatory prerequisite is applicable and unresolved, it must be addressed through legitimate authority rather than recast as `NOT_APPLICABLE` without grounds.

### 4.2 What is **not** automatically required

Conditional S04 research-stage closure does not, by default, require: proving Q1-Q4 universal necessity; proving Q0-Q4 joint sufficiency; resolving F06/F14/F16; executing 62 E0 designs; performing E1 or E2 work; discharging all 24 G03/G06 obligations; adopting INFOPROD, INFOPDS, Information DPP Profile, INFOPCH or PMDD; or granting normative source/schema approval or normative INFOPS release. Separately binding applicable requirements are not excused by this proportionality rule.

## 5. Explicit proposed resolution of C06's safeguards

**Historical C06 remains unchanged.** The owner-approved C06 target `CLOSED_CONDITIONAL_RESEARCH (ONTOLOGY_AND_NORMATIVE_READINESS_UNRESOLVED)` remains `APPROVED_NOT_EFFECTIVE`. C06-F01.6/F01.29 remain governing safeguards for **that** historical decision. The original authoritative charter, amendments and full binding closure criteria have not been authenticated; their existence cannot be denied merely because the examined materials did not recover them.

**A distinct prospective route is proposed, not a retroactive repair.** Acting under the later, owner-approved `INFOPS-GOV-MANDATE-0.1`, the owner may separately adopt new forward-looking research-stage closure criteria. A later owner closure decision, if justified, would operate on the **new instrument's effective basis** and must be explicitly identified as such. It cannot make the earlier C06 target retrospectively effective, silently delete C06's conditions or assert that historical criteria have been verified.

**Material contradiction rule.** If authentic original evidence later establishes a binding prerequisite whose continuing applicability could conflict with the prospective route, that contradiction must be surfaced and competently resolved before a new closure-effect decision. New original-source evidence can justify renewed Path A1 verification only under its own authorization; general V01 suspension is not lifted by this draft.

**Prospective closure remains conditional.** Even after new criteria are adopted, an unresolved material PC failure or an applicable independent mandatory prerequisite prevents a supported closure-effectiveness determination. Unknown historical criteria are not silently converted into known `NOT_APPLICABLE` criteria.

This section is **proposed** PC-03 reconciliation language. It is not yet an adopted interpretation or final disposition of C06.

## 6. Future evaluation discipline (not yet performed)

Each PC criterion must receive one of five explicit statuses in a *subsequent assessment against the adopted instrument*:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `SATISFIED` | Cited admissible evidence and reasoning establish the adopted requirement at its declared assurance level. |
| `NOT_SATISFIED` | Evidence shows an applicable adopted requirement is not met. |
| `NOT_VERIFIED` | Available evidence does not justify satisfaction or an adverse finding. |
| `NOT_APPLICABLE` | A separate competent, reasoned determination establishes that an adopted requirement does not apply; no blanket N/A treatment. |
| `NOT_ASSESSED` | No authorized criterion-level evaluation has been performed. |

**Draft preparation does not count as evaluation.** As of this file, each proposed PC condition remains `NOT_ADOPTED / NOT_ASSESSED`. The owner may accept or revise criteria before adopting them; no outcome can be pre-populated as `SATISFIED` because similar evidence is already known.

Proposed minimum assessment record per PC: criterion ID and adopted version; assertion under assessment; evidence path/decision reference and as-of scope; known gaps; adverse or alternative interpretation; evaluation status with reasons; AI assessment (if used); explicit accountable human disposition; and recorded currentness/applicability. No product/semantic Version or realization occurrence may be inferred from assessment status.

**Prospective closure eligibility logic:** After instrument adoption, an evidence-backed determination is needed for all instrument-validity and research-acceptance requirements PC-01-PC-09. Each applicable criterion must be `SATISFIED`; `NOT_VERIFIED`, `NOT_ASSESSED` and `NOT_SATISFIED` do not count as satisfied. Any `NOT_APPLICABLE` must have separately recorded competence, rationale and non-conflicting authority, and cannot waive an independently mandatory prerequisite. Only after that assessment may PC-10 be fulfilled through an **additional separate closure-effectiveness decision**. This logic is itself **proposed**, not yet a binding rule.
