## 1. Constitutive basis and purpose

KivaNura.org, as declared by its sole human owner, is the governing enterprise for the INFOPS programme. KivaNura.org operates as a single-person micro-enterprise with one human Symbiant. The owner declares and exercises final accountability for internal INFOPS research-stage governance, subject to applicable external obligations and any genuinely independent authority.

This mandate makes that existing owner-led research-governance arrangement explicit **prospectively**. It is a lightweight internal control appropriate to the early `0.1` research maturity of the Information Product Specification. It is not a reconstruction of an earlier charter, a substitute for evidence or a normative qualification specification.

The human owner and AI governance partner may jointly develop findings, challenge assumptions and reconcile evidence. Final approval and accountability remain with the human owner. The AI partner holds no independent legal, institutional or signatory authority, and joint work is not represented as independent external assurance.

## 2. Included decision rights

Within the internal INFOPS research-stage programme, the accountable human owner may:

1. Establish research objectives, investigation boundaries and prospective stage-completion requirements.
2. Propose, approve, amend, reject or defer research findings and governance records.
3. Establish and amend **prospective** research-stage acceptance or conditional-closure criteria through explicit, recorded owner decisions.
4. Evaluate evidence against the applicable, actually approved criteria; preserve uncertainty and adverse findings.
5. Determine future research-stage status transitions through separate accountable decisions, subject to their governing safeguards.
6. Authorize proportionate supporting documentation and traceable repository updates separately from the substantive decisions they record.

An approval of the **power to establish criteria** is not an approval of any particular criteria; an approval of criteria is not proof that they are satisfied; and satisfaction is not itself an effective closure decision.

## 3. Independent jurisdictions and limits

The mandate governs **INFOPS research-stage decisions only**. It does not transfer, merge or override the distinct governing responsibilities of:

- BPS/PIN for generic Productness, Product constitution, identity, Version, Promise and Compound Product authority.
- GEARM/GEARA for Information Proposition, governed meaning, semantic definition/Version and Information architecture.
- The independently governed Data, AI/Intelligence, Service, Creative and other Product-family specifications, notwithstanding shared enterprise ownership.
- BPS DPP Core and applicable Passport issuing, evidence and trust authority.
- UPOS/PFI/HDIP for realization, blueprint binding, compilation, deployment and operational projection.
- Applicable legal, policy and contextual consumption or consequential-action decision-makers.
- The independent A1-A9 determination categories and their legitimate dependencies.

No INFOPS research-stage governance decision can, by itself, establish Product-family qualification, semantic or operational conformance, Passport validity, assurance, maturity, consumption eligibility, consequential-action authorization, source/schema adoption or normative release.

## 4. Proportionate decision and evidence controls

For INFOPS 0.1, the default control sequence is deliberately lightweight:

1. Identify the proposed decision, scope, source evidence, uncertainties and effects.
2. Conduct human-AI deliberation, including adversarial review where appropriate; distinguish AI assessment from original evidence.
3. Record one explicit accountable human owner disposition: `APPROVE`, `AMEND`, `REJECT` or `DEFER`.
4. Preserve a traceable decision record with decision identifier, date, scope, limitations and affected artifacts.
5. For a governing document requiring repository adoption, commit the approved content with a meaningful message and traceable change history.

No external committee, multi-signatory ceremony or mandatory independent audit is created by this mandate. If a particular decision independently requires verification, a separate authority, an independent audit or further evidence, that requirement remains applicable and cannot be waived by the absence of bureaucracy.

Corrections and substantive changes require a further explicit owner disposition and a later traceable commit; prior decision and evidence records must not be silently rewritten.
