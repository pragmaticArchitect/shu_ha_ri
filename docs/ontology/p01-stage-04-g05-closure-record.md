## Consolidated conclusion

The eight owner-approved research findings establish conceptual separation among generic BPS Productness, competing-family qualification, GEARM semantic conformance, realization fidelity, Product-version operational projection, DPP evidence, maturity, assurance, purpose-dependent fitness, Product access, consequential Action authority and normative adoption. Legitimate logical/evidence dependencies remain allowed; decision authority never transfers by implication.

Conditional G05 closure is a bounded assessment that the eight planned owner decisions were reviewed, not proof of universal consistency, exhaustive empirical tests or a necessary-and-sufficient classifier.
