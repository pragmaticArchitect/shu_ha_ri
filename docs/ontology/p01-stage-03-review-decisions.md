## 3. Proposed S04 acceptance checklist

**P01-S04 — Qualification Boundary Reconciliation & P01 Handoff** should not claim readiness until:

1. Owner reviews/adjudicates S02-D01–D06 and S03-D01–D09 (including reject/defer options).
2. Each `Q0–Q5` condition is traced to the correct upstream authority and tested for necessity and sufficiency, including competing families and compound subjects.
3. A source/semantic-version adoption matrix is pinned; no undocumented reinterpretation of GEARM Information or BPS Productness.
4. Data/Information hard cases, editorial Information, AI-assisted Information, knowledge/reference cases and interactive general-purpose analytics are adjudicated with actual Product Promises or deliberately kept undecidable.
5. Independent Productness, family candidate, conformance/currentness, decision/action authorization and PMDD results remain distinct records.
6. An evidence model, ontology/descriptor impact and future P02 INFOPCH transition are defined without converting maturity characteristics into Product qualification gates.
7. Any normative INFOPS rules are explicitly approved and versioned in a *future* release action; research materials remain clearly labeled.
