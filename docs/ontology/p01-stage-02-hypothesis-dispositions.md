## 3. Stage 2 candidate condition set - not a normative classifier

A review team can ask whether a subject is a Product under BPS, whether its **primary promised intrinsic value** is a governed bounded informational proposition, whether its **semantic constitution** carries applicable Information Need/Intent/frame/context/meaning-affecting logic, whether its essential **availability capability** supplies that value to governed consumers, and whether it retains clear **identity invariants** across runtime occurrences and PFI realizations.

But **this is not a completed necessary-and-sufficient definition**: generic BPS Product qualification may require evidence not provided by the cases; the intended boundaries of a "coherent proposition family" remain undecided; and Data/Information and compound examples with overlapping value-bearing essences still challenge uniqueness. A sufficient family classifier must survive intentional attempts to misclassify those cases, with independent source authority.

A central distinction for future rules:

```text
Information can be formed or directly consumed without Productness.
Qualified Productness alone does not determine Product Family.
Analytical semantic constitution does not by itself determine Info family.
Product-family qualification does not confer operational readiness or PMDD maturity.
PFI implementation does not independently determine Product identity or family.
```

## 4. Explicit research disposition of the difficult boundaries

| Boundary | S02 conclusion | Why not close it by assumption? |
|---|---|---|
| **Rich Data Product versus Information Product** | **Unresolved in dual-promise cases** | Same schema, computed VWAP and annotations can serve representation or interpretation. Need actual Product Promise, identity, Constitution and applicable external DPROD/DPDS semantics. |
| **General-purpose analytics service versus Info Product** | **Distinct by leading promise in clear examples; unresolved in configurable bounded services** | Producing Information is not sufficient; a coherent contract for a family of informational propositions may be a real Product. |
| **Human-curated static report** | **Do not reject merely for lack of engine** | Method-dependent meaning survives as constituted value; generic Productness still independent. |
| **AI-assisted / AI-dependent information** | **No automatic AI classification** | AIPS-qualified intelligence constituent may participate without redefining the containing VBE. Material intelligence promises require new assessment. |
| **Multi-proposition / compound offering** | **Unresolved** | Need rule for bounded coherent informational identity versus separately qualified constituent Products or genuine multi-essence Product. |
| **Three enterprise PFI paths** | **Conditional invariance supported** | Same Product Kind can be realized differently; distinct Product instances, controls, eligibility and operating conformance remain separate. |
| **Interpretive logic versus runtime engine** | **Conceptually separated** | Governing semantic logic belongs to meaningful Product definition/constitution; shared execution machinery is an independently governed realization dependency. |

## 5. Proposed P01-S03 exit gates for owner acceptance

1. **Pin source authorities**: exact versions and adopted scope of BPS/PIN, GEARM/GEARA, applicable external DPDS/DPROD and AIPS; no invented source equivalence.
2. **State exact qualification subject**: persistent Product/Version versus report occurrence, built executable, deployment or commercial bundle.
3. **Propose only evidence-backed candidate necessary conditions**: explicit mapping to PIN dimensions and GEARM semantic definitions. Do not conflate BPS generic Productness with Information-family membership.
4. **Attempt sufficiency falsification**: for each proposed conjunction of conditions, explicitly try C02/C21 (enriched Data), C07/C20 (generic service), C12/C31 (compound), C09/C24 (AI value), and C03/C17 (non-Product outputs).
5. **Resolve or explicitly preserve boundedness/compound uncertainty**: decide whether coherent families of propositions require a new Identity Invariant, not an arbitrary metric-count limit.
6. **Preserve method and realization independence**: test constituted/static, human-curated, API, interactive, and AI-assisted forms without an engine ownership rule.
7. **State requalification triggers**: material change in governing proposition, analytic logic/frame/context, primary VBE or essential capability versus benign runtime/hosting substitution.
8. **Separate later concerns**: INFOPCH characteristics/PMDD, Production/consumption authorization, PFI compilation and platform conformity are not family qualification gates.
9. **Validate negative and ambiguous cases**: a classifier must return `UNRESOLVED` when governing Product Promise, family evidence or authoritative version is missing.
10. **Owner sign-off**: acknowledge that S02 is an investigatory review, and approve a named S03 drafting scope rather than labeling S02 itself as a normative release.
