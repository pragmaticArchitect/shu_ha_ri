# Information Product qualification boundary

> **P01 Stage 3 candidate rules published for owner review; qualification remains UNRESOLVED and NON-NORMATIVE.**

See [the authority and ontology baseline](./p01-stage-01-authority-and-ontology-baseline.md) and [challenge-case inventory](./p01-stage-01-classification-cases.md).

Questions for P01:

- Is the subject a Product under BPS/PIN? (Generic Product qualification.)
- If qualified, is the Value-Bearing Essence intrinsically Information rather than Data, AI/Intelligence, a Service or a presentation artifact?
- Which Information Proposition and consumer value support family qualification?
- Which identity, constitution and family invariants apply?
- Which examples should be rejected, accepted or held unresolved?

Maturity scoring must not answer these qualification questions. Product creation must be intentional; analytics, publication, reuse or discovery alone must not confer Productness.


## Stage 2 research status

[Stage 2 research](./p01-stage-02-pin-differentiation-falsification.md) rejects engine ownership, AI participation, data enrichment, presentation medium, numerical payload equality and PMDD maturity as standalone family qualifications. The [36-case ledger](./p01-stage-02-case-disposition-ledger.md) retains Product Promise-dependent Data/Information and compound cases as unresolved. No conjunction of criteria is yet adopted as necessary and sufficient for INFOPS Product-family certification.

## Stage 3 conditional qualification proposal

[Candidate Q0-Q5 rules](./p01-stage-03-candidate-qualification-rules.md) separate independently qualified BPS Productness (Q0), informational VBE/semantic constitution/EVC/identity (Q1-Q4) and competing-family/compound adjudication (Q5). The [decision procedure](./p01-stage-03-decision-procedure.md) and [20 hypothetical boundary cases](./p01-stage-03-boundary-casework.md) are research aids, not a normative or executable classifier. **Joint sufficiency remains unproven** and S03 has not been owner-approved.
