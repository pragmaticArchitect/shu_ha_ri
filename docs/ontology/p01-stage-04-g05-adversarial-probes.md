### Interpretation framework

- **Prohibited substitution:** a finding mistakenly treated as establishing an independently governed determination.
- **Legitimate dependency:** shared evidence or a prerequisite without authority transfer.
- **Compatible divergence:** different conclusions about different scopes, Versions, subjects or dimensions.
- **Unresolved:** evidence or jurisdiction insufficient for a definitive conclusion.
- **Potential substantive contradiction:** materially incompatible, adequately supported conclusions under identical governing scope and criteria.

These labels explain research reasoning; they are **not** a normative INFOPS status vocabulary. Exact subjects, semantic Versions, realization, occurrence, evidence, authority and currentness remain mandatory interpretive distinctions where material.
