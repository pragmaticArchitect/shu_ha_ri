## Adversarial research method to propose at G04-D01

For the **exact assessed subject and Version**, establish the bounded Product Promise, independently evidenced BPS Productness Q0, primary intrinsic value (VBE) and essential capability (EVC), governed meaning/constitution, consumer value contract, Product identity/version invariants, and the *strongest competing family interpretation*. Identify independent Data/AI/Service/Creative/Compound authority, evidence limitations and accountable disposition without equating tool, deployment, passport or bundle with a Product.

Use positive, negative, near-miss and contradictory examples. Challenge **necessity** by finding legitimately qualified Information Products that seem to fail a proposed Q1-Q4 condition. Challenge **sufficiency** by finding subjects that satisfy proposed Q0-Q4 but retain material Data, AI, Service, Creative or Compound interpretations. Q5 must remain a review gate, not an ontological predicate.

## High-value ambiguous cases

- **C02/C21:** Enriched Data and VWAP-bearing Data Products versus independently promised Information. Same numeric payload and semantics do not prove Family; exact Promise and relevant Data Product authority decide what can be assessed.
- **C07/C20:** General-purpose analytics and computational services versus one governed informational proposition or coherent family.
- **C09/C24/C33:** AI Product Promise versus Information produced or assisted by AI, including genuine Promise evolution.
- **C05/C06/C19/C30:** Static, human-curated or editorially governed Information without a mandatory owned or running engine.
- **C12/C18/C31:** One coherent informational Product, dependencies, several separately constituted Products, commercial bundle or potentially Compound Product. BPS Compound treatment is not sufficiently pinned to pre-certify an answer.
- **C15/C16/C27/C34:** Identical output values with changed semantics; preserve historical definition Versions and requalification triggers.
- **C22/C25/C28/C32/C35/C36:** Different engines, tenants, realizations, occurrences and parameters do not automatically create or merge Product identities.

## Research status vocabulary only

Keep `PROVISIONAL_INFO_CANDIDATE`, `COMPETING_FAMILY`, `PRODUCTNESS_NOT_SHOWN`, `BOUNDARY_UNRESOLVED`, `INVARIANCE_SUPPORTED`, `SEMANTIC_CHANGE`, `IDENTITY_OR_OCCURRENCE`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` and `CONFLICT_REVIEW` as research-level outcomes. They are not qualification or conformance certificates. Never invent an external authority's affirmative Product Family decision from a hypothetical INFOPS case.

## Proposed G04 exit evidence

1. Independently evaluate competing Product-family interpretations rather than merely classifying by output type.
2. Preserve Productness Q0 and the Q1-Q4 provisional Information-specific criteria; Q5 remains procedural.
3. Adjudicate or explicitly retain unresolved status for C02/C21 and C12/C18/C31.
4. Test both falsifiable necessity and sufficiency, with false-positive, false-negative and inconclusive cases.
5. Distinguish Product identity, Version, occurrence, realization and operational conformance.
6. Carry unresolved source/adoption and Compound Product questions to G05-G07 without forced closure.
7. Record each substantive finding under a distinct owner disposition before proceeding to the next decision.
