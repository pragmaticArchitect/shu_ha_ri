# Information Product ontology - P01 in progress

**P01 Stage 1 (preserved working draft)** establishes the inherited authority baseline, classification hypotheses and a challenge-case inventory. It publishes **no normative Information Product qualification rules**.

Start with [P01 Stage 1: ontology and authority baseline](./p01-stage-01-authority-and-ontology-baseline.md) and the [classification challenge cases](./p01-stage-01-classification-cases.md).

The core unresolved challenge is the intrinsic distinction between qualified Data, Information, AI, Service and Compound Products **without deriving family status from source technology, format or Product maturity**. [Qualification boundary planning](./qualification-boundary.md) remains open until later P01 stages.

## Stage 1 ontology review

The non-normative [Stage 1 ontology review and hypothesis dispositions](./p01-stage-01-ontology-review.md) examines H01-H10 in light of the Product identity, analytical constitution, PFI realization and dependency-boundary discussions. The accompanying [realization and Product-family stress tests](./p01-stage-01-realization-stress-tests.md) extend C01-C18 with cross-enterprise and lifecycle cases. Neither page adopts family qualification rules; the baseline hypotheses remain intact until reviewed.


## Stage 2 - PIN differentiation and falsification

The new [Stage 2 research baseline](./p01-stage-02-pin-differentiation-falsification.md) challenges over-broad Product-family proxies using the [36-case disposition ledger](./p01-stage-02-case-disposition-ledger.md), [three-enterprise PFI controlled experiments](./p01-stage-02-cross-enterprise-realization-tests.md) and [hypothesis decisions / S03 gates](./p01-stage-02-hypothesis-dispositions.md). The original files are **historical proposed research**, now followed by [six explicit owner-approved S02 research decisions](./p01-stage-02-owner-approved-decisions.md). The approvals are **not** normative Information Product family criteria. The original S01 hypotheses and stress cases are retained for traceability.

## Stage 3 - Conditional candidate qualification rules

[Candidate intrinsic rules (Q0-Q5)](./p01-stage-03-candidate-qualification-rules.md), [review decision procedure](./p01-stage-03-decision-procedure.md), [20 boundary probes](./p01-stage-03-boundary-casework.md), [PFI invariance protocol](./p01-stage-03-pfi-semantic-invariance.md), and [historical owner decision proposals](./p01-stage-03-review-decisions.md), subsequently [approved as nine research decisions](./p01-stage-03-owner-approved-decisions.md), develop a possible necessary-and-sufficient target **without claiming joint sufficiency or Product certification**. The [original and refined crude-oil-to-driver analogy](../foundations/crude-oil-to-driver-teaching-analogy.md) is recorded as an informative teaching model; it does not redefine GEARM or create a mandatory Data → Information → Intelligence pipeline.
