#### G04-D01-F01 - Competing-Family Adjudication Protocol, Exact Product Subject and Evidence Discipline {#g04-d01-f01}

**Owner disposition:** `APPROVE` (20 October 2026). **Status:** conditional research / non-normative.

**Research summary:** Establish an exact-subject and evidence-bounded procedure for competing-family research; Q5 is procedural and Q0 remains independently BPS-governed.

**Original S02 case dispositions preserved:** `C03` `PRODUCTNESS_NOT_SHOWN`, `C14` `PRODUCTNESS_NOT_SHOWN`, `C17` `PRODUCTNESS_NOT_SHOWN`, `C29` `PRODUCTNESS_NOT_SHOWN`.

- **Review purpose.** G04 investigates competing Product-family interpretations and Compound candidates adversarially; it is research, not certification.
- **Exact subject.** Identify assessed subject, Product/Version, scope, governing Promise, accountable authority, constituents, dependencies, realizations and informational occurrences before classification.
- **Productness precedence.** Examine independent BPS/PIN Productness as Q0 before making definitive Information-family claims; INFOPS does not award Productness.
- **Candidate intrinsic conditions.** Q1-Q4 remain investigatory differentiators with universal necessity and joint sufficiency unproven.
- **Q5 is procedural.** Competing-family and Compound review is a procedural/evidential gate and not an intrinsic Product property.
- **Independent jurisdiction.** Compare Data, AI, Service, Creative, other and Compound interpretations through their independently applicable authorities, not locally invented certification.
- **Promise and PIN.** Investigate governed Product Promise, VBE, EVC, constitution and identity rather than technical labels, formats, hosting or marketing categories.
- **Multi-promise restraint.** Where separately material Promises or essences are credible, preserve separate subjects and possible Compound interpretation rather than forcing a single Family.
- **Dependency non-inheritance.** A Product does not inherit the intrinsic Family of consumed or constituting capabilities merely because it depends on them.
- **Semantic fidelity.** Evaluate meaning-affecting context, assumptions, method, population, temporal rules and Promise separately from numerical equality.
- **Realization invariance.** Different eligible PFI paths, runtime, publication medium, hosting and AI assistance do not independently determine Family when governing commitments remain equivalent.
- **Falsification.** Use positive, negative, near-miss and conflicting cases to challenge necessity of individual conditions and sufficiency of their conjunction.
- **Evidence discipline.** Distinguish observed facts, governing claims, inferred interpretations, assumptions, counterarguments, source/Version references, gaps and reviewer provenance.
- **Fail-closed uncertainty.** Do not issue definitive qualification where Productness, primary Promise, source authorities or Compound boundaries remain unresolved.
- **Independent assessments.** Keep Productness, family, INFOPROD, INFOPDS, HPD, DPP, conformance, readiness, maturity, issuance and authorization determinations separate.
- **Source and release boundary.** Carry all G03 adoption and compatibility holds; approving the method does not authorize normative INFOPS qualification or publication.
