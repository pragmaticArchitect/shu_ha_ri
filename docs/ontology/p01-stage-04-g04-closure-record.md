## Independent semantic/Product jurisdictions

- **BPS/PIN:** generic Productness, Product Promise, identity/Kind/Family, Compound authority.
- **GEDRM/GEDRA and applicable Data authorities:** Data semantics, Data Product qualification and Data architecture.
- **GEARM/GEARA:** Information/Analytics semantic meaning and analytical architecture.
- **AIPS/GEAIRM/GEAIRA:** AI Product qualification, enterprise AI semantics and architecture.
- **BOPS/GRNPS and relevant Creative specifications:** independent creative Book/Graphic Novel Product jurisdictions.
- **UPOS/HDIP-CLA/PFI:** generic Productization, governed realization and operational projections, not Product qualification authority.
- **INFOPS:** proposed Information Product specialization, subject to future normative adoption.

Potential Data/Information, Service/Information, AI/Information, Creative/Information and Compound/single-Family overlap questions remain unresolved wherever applicable authority or exact Product evidence is insufficient.

## Artifact and assessment boundaries

- **INFOPROD:** prospective semantic Information Product descriptor, not adopted.
- **INFOPDS:** prospective Information Product realization/deployment specification, not adopted.
- **AIPROD/AIPDS and other family descriptors:** retain independent family-native authority.
- **HDIP HPD:** operational Product-Version projection, not Product Family qualification.
- **BPS DPP Core:** conceptually cross-family trust/passport framework; source Version/namespace adoption unresolved.
- **Information DPP Profile:** future specialization, no issued normative schema or passport qualification.
Independent Productness, Family qualification, operational/semantic conformance, passport trust/issuance, maturity, assurance, authorization and publication are not interchangeable.
