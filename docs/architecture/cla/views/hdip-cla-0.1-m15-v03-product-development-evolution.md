# HDIP-CLA 0.1 — M15-V03 Product Development & Evolution (PDEP)

**Status:** Presentation-only illustration of **M15-V03**; not a new normative lifecycle specification.

## Shift creation power left

**From requesting Products to creating Products.** The **Symbiant** is the diagram's experience-level term for the accountable Product Owner/participant. It does **not** establish a new canonical architectural role or replace applicable Product, Data/source, policy or consequential Decision/Action authorities.

The Symbiant declares and refines Product purpose, value, use, business expectations, design and evolution intent. HDIP supplies governed discovery, compatibility resolution, compilation, realization/deployment coordination, publication and evidence automation. Accountable judgement remains with the appropriate participant or independently authorized party.

**Self-service ≠ self-authorization.** Platform automation does not confer Product qualification, enterprise investment authority, source authority, entitlement or consequential Decision/Action authorization. Nor does self-service require that specialist assistance be prohibited; it means routine creation is **not structurally dependent on an intermediary Delivery team**.

## Interpreting the experience groupings

The seven labelled areas in the illustration are **non-normative experience groupings**, not a newly prescribed seven-stage PDEP lifecycle and **not** P1–P5 Plane identifiers. They show how the Product Owner may move through guided intent and design, dependency/policy compatibility checks, automation, decision support, publication and continuous evolution. Occurrences can branch, reuse governed state, iterate or re-enter at the earliest materially affected lifecycle point.

| Architectural region | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| P1 — Governed Intent, Definition & Authority Context | Product purpose, governed definitions, semantics and accountable context without annexing external authorities |
| P2 — Discovery, Composition & Realization | Governed discovery, dependency/compatibility resolution, validation, compilation and binding |
| P3 — Runtime, Formation & Execution | Typed Data, Information or Intelligence realization, preserving their different semantics |
| P4 — Provision, Consumption & Integration | Governed publication, exposure and consumption hand-off to SSCF/CEP; publishing does not grant entitlement |
| P5 — Outcome, Value & Evolution | Observe outcome/effectiveness/value signals, preserve Product-version history and drive governed re-entry |
| X1–X9 | Nine cross-cutting governance, evidence, assurance, cost and lifecycle responsibilities, applicable according to context |
| HDIP-FND | Shared Utility/Foundation enabling realization; not a semantic, Product or financial authority |

## Data Product canonical specialization — Stage 0 plus Stages 1–10

The separate lower band represents **only** the frozen detailed **Data Product** PDEP specialization from M10. The eleven numbered stages are: Preconditions; Intent Record; Authority Confirmation; Policy Intent; Semantic Descriptor (DPROD Draft); Business Assertions / Evidence Rules; Usage Contract; Product Preview (Abstract); Compilation; Deployment (DPDS Generation & Execution); and DPROD Finalization & Registration.

Stage 8 uses **RRG, HPD, conditional SYNCTX, RGB, Realization Plan, Blueprint Binding and Compilation Record**. Stage 10 distinguishes Product Registry registration, Catalog/Marketplace projection and governed interface exposure. G0, Spec A and Spec C remain synchronized presentation projections rather than competing lifecycle definitions.

**Information, Intelligence/AI and Compound Products** retain their applicable family-authoritative obligations and lifecycle specializations; Data Stages 0–10 are **not** imposed universally.

## Economic transparency without prescribed investment governance

The guided experience should make **indicative cost and economic impact** visible early enough to inform decisions, with more reliable estimates as dependencies and realization become clearer. **X9 — Economic & Cost Accountability** supports economic evidence; **P5** distinguishes actual consumption/cost from outcomes and value.

HDIP **does not prescribe** prefunding, budgets, funding committees, approval workflows, investment thresholds or delegation to agentic AI. Those are choices for the **adopting enterprise's architecture, governance and realization Profiles**, under applicable external financial and policy authorities. The diagram's readiness area is decision support, **not a universal funding-approval Gate**, and estimates are not final-bill guarantees.

## Preservation of authority and visual status

The illustration preserves the frozen M17 semantic, Product-family, source, legal/policy and consequential Decision/Action boundaries. PDEP is a **cross-Plane lifecycle**, not a sixth Plane, a project-driven waterfall, a mandatory synchronous pipeline or a prescribed deployment topology. Visual success does not establish M16 architectural conformance.
