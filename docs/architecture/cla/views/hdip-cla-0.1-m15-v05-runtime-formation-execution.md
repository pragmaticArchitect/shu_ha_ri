## One runtime Plane, three canonical ABBs

- **HDIP-P3-ABB-01 — Data Processing & Movement Runtime Boundary:** governed acquisition/binding, transformation, derivation, movement/materialization and typed Data runtime evidence.
- **HDIP-P3-ABB-02 — Information Formation & Analytical Runtime Boundary:** governed calculation, Metric/Indicator/Threshold evaluation where applicable, formation/synthesis of analytical Information and typed evidence.
- **HDIP-P3-ABB-03 — Intelligence Execution Runtime Boundary:** governed execution of applicable learned, symbolic, knowledge-based, planning, hybrid or agent/tool mechanisms with execution evidence and applicable GEAIRA-aligned authority boundaries.

The three ABBs **are peers inside one P3 Plane**, not successive Data → Information → Intelligence processing stages. A common realization may share compute, storage, messaging or orchestration but must retain the typed responsibilities and independent semantic jurisdictions. Runtime success does **not** create Product qualification or independent Decision/Action authority.

## P1 / P2 / P4 / P5 relationships

- **P1:** authoritative meaning, intent/purpose, accountability and governing runtime obligations, including **INT-02** Governing Runtime Envelope (P1 → P3).
- **P2:** discover, resolve, validate, compile and bind governed realizations, with **INT-09** P2 → P3 realization binding and **INT-10** P3 → P2 runtime readiness/binding feedback. Historical PFI is a helpful experience/capability reference, not a new Plane or ABB.
- **P4:** consumer-facing discovery, selection, access, consumption and downstream integration. **INT-17** P4 → P3 invokes typed runtime responsibilities; **INT-18** P3 → P4 carries results/effects. P2 retains governed discovery/resolution while P4 mediates consumer-facing discovery through Marketplace, search/browse, Concierge or agent-mediated experiences.
- **P5:** interprets runtime outcome/effectiveness and P4 consumption evidence (**INT-19** P3 → P5; **INT-20** P4 → P5) and supports feedback and governed evolution, including **INT-07** P5 → P1, **INT-15** P5 → P2 and **INT-21** P5 → P4.

The named arrows are **selected M12 interaction references**, not a mandatory synchronous execution topology. The diagram does not prescribe runtime/provider topology or a one-to-one deployed-service mapping.

## Discovery supports both consumption and intentional Product creation

A Consumer may discover and select an existing Product solely to **access, browse, query, analyse or visualize** its available content or capability, without constituting another Product. Alternatively, discovery or exploration may motivate the accountable participant to enter **PDEP** for intentional creation/evolution of a richer Product. **Both** paths may use metadata-based, semantic, Concierge or agent-mediated discovery.

The distinction is **purpose and governed Product constitution**, not the choice of search interface. Product access, discovery, composition or output reuse never creates Product qualification automatically. Agent mediation neither replaces semantic authority nor confers entitlement, financial authority or consequential Decision/Action authorization. For the detailed consumer lifecycle, refer to [M11 — SSCF / CEP](/docs/architecture/cla/hdip-cla-0.1-m11-sscf-cep-architecture) and forthcoming M15-V06.

## Cross-cutting obligations, evidence and Foundation

All **X1–X9** Overlays remain applicable according to context. Particularly relevant runtime responsibilities include X3 provenance, X4 observability, X5 fitness/quality, X6 evaluation and assurance, X7 version/currentness, X8 resilience and X9 cost. Evidence, assurance, cost and value must not be collapsed into one score or equated automatically.

**HDIP-FND** supplies technology/utility enablement without establishing Product qualification, semantic authority, policy ownership or Decision/Action authority. **PDEP, CEP/SSCF, historical PFI/DPFI and COVP** are lifecycle/experience/historical constructs spanning existing Planes and Overlays, not further canonical Planes.

This diagram is a governed **presentation projection of M15-V05**. It does not modify M01–M17, A1–A15 or any enterprise-specific realization/financial governance choice.
