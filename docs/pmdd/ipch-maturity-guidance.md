## Core criteria and enterprise assessment profiles

Characteristic guidance defines **mandatory assessment claims**, their meaning, positive and negative criteria, applicable evidence expectations, and evidence-limited findings. Enterprises may select implementation mechanisms, manual or automated execution and appropriately stringent evidence/assurance profiles. They must not silently remove or redefine mandatory characteristic claims while asserting the same maturity conformance.

For IPCH09, `CBD-M01–CBD-M08` are mandatory conjunctive criteria, `CBD-N01–CBD-N09` negative conditions, and `CBD-ES01–CBD-ES08` claim-specific evidence. Preserve federated policy authority and design-time visibility, Product/Version binding, executable policies, actual runtime enforcement, consistent Product-level control coverage and violations evidence. DPCH09 four-layer and AIPCH10 three-layer hierarchies remain distinct; integrated authorized human oversight is not a substitute for required computational controls. Derived Information Product obligations require independent competent policy/legal authority. DPP is optional supporting evidence, not a universal conformance requirement. `CBD-R01–CBD-R08` remain OPEN and `CBD-A01–CBD-A12` UNEXECUTED. For IPCH08, `OBS-M01–OBS-M08` are mandatory conjunctive criteria; `OBS-N01–OBS-N09` negative conditions; `OBS-ES01–OBS-ES08` claim-specific evidence. Continuous Product-level operational/informational SLA–SLO observability and recurring **executed automated enterprise PMDD maturity monitoring** are distinct required dimensions. No unexecuted E0 designs or draft PMDD rules establish monitoring execution. `OBS-R02`, `OBS-R03`, `OBS-R05` and all `OBS-R01–OBS-R08` remain OPEN; anti-circularity and Product/Version/evaluator-version traceability apply. For IPCH07, `REU-M01–REU-M07` are mandatory conjunctive reuse claims, `REU-N01–REU-N08` negative conditions, and `REU-ES01–REU-ES07` evidence requirements. Both structurally generalized capability and actual independent adoption are required for `MET`; `REU-R02` (narrow/regulatory scope) and `REU-R08` (producer-assisted adoption) remain OPEN. For IPCH06, `TRU-M01–TRU-M07` are mandatory conjunctive Product-level trust claims, `TRU-N01–TRU-N08` are negative conditions, and `TRU-ES01–TRU-ES07` are evidence requirements. DPCH06 DPP canonical-trust-carrier and AIPCH07 DPP-or-governed-equivalent positions remain distinct; INFOPS equivalence/conformance is not established (`TRU-R03` OPEN). For IPCH05, `SDC-M01–SDC-M07` are mandatory conjunctive self-description claims, `SDC-N01–SDC-N08` are negative conditions, and `SDC-ES01–SDC-ES07` are evidence requirements. INFOPROD / applicable DPP are examples of Product-bound descriptive artifacts, not mandated DPP implementation or conformance. For IPCH04, `DIS-M01–DIS-M06` are mandatory conjunctive discovery criteria, `DIS-N01–DIS-N07` are negative conditions and `DIS-ES01–DIS-ES06` are evidence requirements. For IPCH03, `DEC-M01–DEC-M06` are the mandatory conjunctive declarative claims, `DEC-N01–DEC-N07` are negative criteria and `DEC-ES01–DEC-ES06` are evidence requirements. For IPCH02, `DEP-M01–DEP-M06` are the mandatory conjunctive self-service claims, `DEP-N01–DEP-N07` are failure conditions, and `DEP-ES01–DEP-ES06` define evidence expectations. No individual-criterion averaging is permitted. For IPCH01, `OWN-M01–OWN-M05` are the proposed mandatory core ownership claims; `OWN-N01–OWN-N07` describe failure indicators and `ES-D01–ES-D07` define evidence controls. The guide provides a qualitative finding rule before the D03 experimental score mapping (`MET=1`, `PARTIAL=0.5`, `NOT_MET=0`). Missing or contradictory evidence is not automatically `NOT_MET`, and criterion-level averaging is not authorized.

**Source and qualification boundaries.** IPCH maturity does not establish BPS/PIN Productness, Information Product family qualification, GEARM/GEARA authority, policy approval, deployment eligibility or release readiness.
