## Core criteria and enterprise assessment profiles

Characteristic guidance defines **mandatory assessment claims**, their meaning, positive and negative criteria, applicable evidence expectations, and evidence-limited findings. Enterprises may select implementation mechanisms, manual or automated execution and appropriately stringent evidence/assurance profiles. They must not silently remove or redefine mandatory characteristic claims while asserting the same maturity conformance.

For IPCH01, `OWN-M01–OWN-M05` are the proposed mandatory core ownership claims; `OWN-N01–OWN-N07` describe failure indicators and `ES-D01–ES-D07` define evidence controls. The guide provides a qualitative finding rule before the D03 experimental score mapping (`MET=1`, `PARTIAL=0.5`, `NOT_MET=0`). Missing or contradictory evidence is not automatically `NOT_MET`, and criterion-level averaging is not authorized.

**Source and qualification boundaries.** IPCH maturity does not establish BPS/PIN Productness, Information Product family qualification, GEARM/GEARA authority, policy approval, deployment eligibility or release readiness. See the [IPCH catalogue](../infopch/overview.md) and [IPCH01 evidence/status record](../infopch/ipch01-research-status.md).
