# Human, deterministic and AI Agent PMDD assessment guidance

> **Experimental E0 research, not an operative assessment standard.** INFOPS D02/D03 approved bounded assessment and scoring proposals. The IPCH01 Domain-Owned guidance is a prepared DRAFT-02 with G2 PASS and G3 PASS_WITH_QUALIFICATIONS. G4–G6 have not been executed and no empirical Information Product assessments are established.

Start from the **[PMDD IPCH Maturity Guidance catalogue](./ipch-maturity-guidance.md)** to access the individual criteria, including **[IPCH01 Domain-Owned](../infopch/IPCH01.md)**. The [Agent Guidance landing page](/INFOPCH-PMDD-guidance) describes automation's role. The same characteristic meanings apply regardless of assessment mechanism.

## Experimental assessment discipline

1. Establish independent assessment eligibility and the **exact Product/Version**. A merely provisional experimental profile match must meet the approved D02 conditions; this guide cannot itself qualify a Product.
2. Establish characteristic and obligation applicability. Whole-characteristic N/A requires competent, affirmative justification; lack of an owner is not an IPCH01 N/A condition.
3. Examine each mandatory core claim using current, claim-specific establishing, corroborating and contrary evidence; preserve provenance, subject alignment and uncertainty.
4. Apply the individual guidance's **qualitative decision rules**: `MET`, `PARTIAL`, `NOT_MET`, or evidence-justified `NOT_APPLICABLE`. `EVIDENCE_LIMITED` is a qualifier and cannot be treated as a fifth maturity result or an automatic zero.
5. Apply the experimental D03 mapping **only after** a characteristic-level finding is defensible: `MET=1`, `PARTIAL=0.5`, `NOT_MET=0`, equal characteristic weights, valid N/A exclusions and bounded unresolved evidence as approved. Do not average the internal IPCH01 core criteria or invent IPCH L0/L1/L2 maturity bands.
6. Preserve separate critical-governance and assurance determinations and record the finding, reasoning, evidence, limitations and assessment time for competent review.

## Enterprise choice without semantic dilution

Enterprises may choose implementation tools, documentation formats, collection workflows, audit frequency and proportionate or more stringent corroboration policies. They may **not** silently eliminate the characteristic's mandatory claims, reinterpret a foundational ownership failure as Met or use an agent to manufacture institutional authority. Profile choices and assessment evidence should be traceable.

**Not yet established:** empirical consistency, an adopted assessment profile, full upstream source audit, any final IPCH01 guide approval, G4 adversarial results or normative INFOPS maturity certification.
