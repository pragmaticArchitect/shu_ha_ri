# Information Product Specification (INFOPS)

> **Status — INFOPS-P01 Stage 3 CONDITIONAL QUALIFICATION DRAFT (NON-NORMATIVE).** This site is under active ontology/authority analysis. It is **not** an approved INFOPS 0.1 specification, Product qualification framework, conformance standard, or maturity catalogue.

INFOPS is intended to define what qualifies as an **Information Product**, consistent with the generic Product semantics of **BPS/PIN** and analytical meaning defined by **GEARM**.

The work will separately develop Information Product characteristics (**INFOPCH**) and evidence-based **Product Maturity-Driven Development (PMDD)** guidance for human and machine assessors.

Review [Stage 3 candidate qualification rules](./ontology/p01-stage-03-candidate-qualification-rules.md), the [proposed decision procedure](./ontology/p01-stage-03-decision-procedure.md), [20 additional boundary cases](./ontology/p01-stage-03-boundary-casework.md), and KivaNura's [refined oil-to-driver teaching analogy](./foundations/crude-oil-to-driver-teaching-analogy.md). The prior [Stage 2 falsification](./ontology/p01-stage-02-pin-differentiation-falsification.md) and its [36-case ledger](./ontology/p01-stage-02-case-disposition-ledger.md) remain intact. For source baseline, start with the [programme and roadmap](./specification/roadmap.md), [authority map](./foundations/authority-map.md), [P01 Stage 1 baseline](./ontology/p01-stage-01-authority-and-ontology-baseline.md), and [classification cases](./ontology/p01-stage-01-classification-cases.md).

The HDIP DII reference implementation is a downstream companion workstream, **not** part of the normative qualification definition.
