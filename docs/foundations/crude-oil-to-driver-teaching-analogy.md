# KivaNura's crude-oil-to-driver teaching analogy

> **INFORMATIVE EDUCATIONAL MODEL — NON-NORMATIVE.** This page records KivaNura's original classroom analogy and the ontological refinement agreed during INFOPS-P01. It is not a GEARM, GEDRM, GEAIRM, BPS/PIN, AIPS or INFOPS semantic definition, a Product-family classifier, or a mandatory Data-to-Information-to-Intelligence production sequence. The original imagery is retained alongside explicit limits to prevent the metaphor from being interpreted literally.

## 1. Original instructional analogy — preserved conceptually

KivaNura uses the automotive journey from crude oil to the driver to help students recognize distinctions between Data, Information, Knowledge and Intelligence.

| Element | Original teaching characterization | Oil and automotive image | Intended point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Data** | Raw material | **Crude oil** | Facts/observations are available from sources and may need organizing or cleansing before a particular task. |
| **Curated Data** | Structured material | **Refined oil** | Prepared, standardized and governed representations are more readily usable. |
| **Information** | Contextualized material | **Fuel in motion**, notably combustion and a dashboard reading | Purpose, context and applied logic give observations useful meaning, illustrated by **“Speed: 70 mph; engine overheating.”** |
| **Knowledge** | Structured experience | **Roadmap and manual** | Retained learning, experience and understanding help interpret information and navigate situations. |
| **Intelligence** | Active capability | **Driver or pilot** | Human or autonomous capability interprets conditions, learns, reasons, adapts and may steer, brake or accelerate. |

Original teaching contrast:

- **Information is a state of Data** — Data given meaning and context.
- **Intelligence is an active capability** — the power to process and apply meaning, including in real time.
- Without fuel, the driver is stranded; without a driver, fuel in an idling engine does not accomplish the intended journey.

These sentences are retained as **pedagogical language**, not adopted literally as semantic axioms. In particular, the metaphor of fuel being burned is an illustration of purposeful transformation/use, not a definition of Information.

## 2. Refined ontology: separate meaning from physical process

| Topic | Refined teaching explanation | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Data | Data **represents** observations or subject matter and may be raw, processed, curated or meaningfully structured. | Raw Data is not necessarily chaotic, unusable or devoid of semantics. Curated Data is not inherently passive. |
| Information | Information is **meaning-bearing content** established through interpretation of Data, observations, evidence or existing Information under an applicable frame and context. | Engine combustion is a *process*, while a relevant interpreted dashboard indication is the better analogue for Information. GEARM owns this semantic meaning. |
| Knowledge | Knowledge is retained or available understanding useful for interpretation; it may be tacit, explicit, represented, learned or externally documented. | The manual is a *representation of knowledge*, not the entirety of knowledge itself. Knowledge can influence information formation. |
| Intelligence | Intelligence is the **capacity** to perceive, infer, reason, adapt, plan or otherwise use understanding where applicable. | The driver is one realization. Intelligence does not always imply AI, autonomy or authorization to act. AIPS/GEAIRM own applicable intelligence/AI semantics. |
| Product | A **qualified Product** has its own BPS/PIN identity and Promise, irrespective of whether it uses oil, sensors, Data Products, Information Products or AI Products. | Having Information or intelligence somewhere in an application does not qualify that application as an Information or AI Product. |

**Sharper student-facing line:**

> **Data represents. Information conveys interpreted meaning. Knowledge supports retained understanding. Intelligence is a capacity to reason, adapt and respond.**

This is an accessible educational distinction, not a claim that each term is universally exclusive or that they must occur in one order.

## 3. Interpret a dashboard observation with context

1. **Observation:** a sensor reports temperature `112 °C`, engine RPM and a timestamp. These are data representations.
2. **Interpretive frame:** assess whether engine thermal conditions are safe under the current vehicle's operating limits and journey circumstances.
3. **Context:** vehicle type, sensor calibration, road grade, outside temperature, load, period and threshold effective date.
4. **Information:** “The engine exceeds its permitted operating temperature for this applicable context.” The meaningful assessment depends on governing definitions and rules.
5. **Knowledge:** prior operating experience and relevant manuals may explain that overheating is more likely during heavy climbs, or help evaluate alternatives.
6. **Intelligence:** a human or software capability may infer a risk and recommend appropriate responses; authorization to brake, stop, or take consequential action is separately governed.

The driver may also reason from visual observation, traffic rules, experience, or communicated instructions. Not every instance of intelligence depends on first receiving a separately productized Information Product.

## 4. The educational arrow is NOT a universal workflow

```text
Explanatory learning progression:
Data  ~>  Information  ~>  Knowledge  ~>  Intelligence

Actual semantic/operational relationships:
- Knowledge and contextual assumptions can guide Information formation.
- Intelligence can form Information, including by invoking governed inputs.
- Information can be reused, copied and interpreted without material depletion.
- Data Products may be consumed directly without first becoming Information Products.
- A Product can depend on Data, Information or AI Products without changing its own family.
- Recommendation, Decision, Action and Outcome remain distinct; capabilities do not confer authorization.
```

The model does not require HDIP's Data, Information and Intelligence Product families to be arranged as mandatory successive processing tiers, and it does not impose an Information Product analytical engine inside the Product boundary.

## 5. ProductVerse examples from the teaching metaphor

| Product candidate | Primary Promise for evaluation | Important distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle telemetry Data Product | Governed representation of vehicle observations/events | Metric fields or semantic definitions alone do not necessarily change Data Product family. |
| Engine-health Information Product | Governed contextual interpretation of thermal health under defined thresholds and conditions | Requires an independently qualified Product and informational Promise; any engine can be a shared enterprise service. |
| Predictive-failure AI Product | Qualified intelligence capability for failure likelihood inference, subject to AIPS | Can be used by the Information Product or car without changing either containing Product automatically. |
| Automotive Product | Transport and vehicle functionality | Can contain or invoke all three without turning into an AI Product by dependency inheritance. |

All four labels above are **illustrative candidates**, not actual qualification findings. The same sensor values might be used in all four without making them one intrinsic Product Family.

## 6. How INFOPS uses this analogy

The analogy supports two distinct claims:

- **Semantic requirement:** an informational promise requires an interpretive or analytical lens—applicable intent, frame, context and meaning-affecting logic—so consumers can understand its meaning.
- **Realization independence:** the physical/computational engine performing that logic may be selected by PFI through eligible golden paths, shared across Products or sourced externally; it is not an intrinsic family test.

These are *conceptual inputs* to the [P01-S03 candidate rules](../ontology/p01-stage-03-candidate-qualification-rules.md), not substitutes for independent BPS Productness, GEARM authority, competing-family evaluation or evidence. The formal boundary remains **Information Proposition != Information Product**.

## 7. Authority and citation alignment (before any normative adoption)

- **GEDRM/GEDRA:** generic Data semantics; the “crude oil” image does not redefine Data.
- **GEARM 0.1, sections 3.5–3.9 and related semantics:** interpretation, Analytical Logic, contextual Information, Information Proposition, and distinct definitions/occurrences.
- **GEARA:** direct analytical consumption versus intentional Information Product provision; no mandatory Productization.
- **BPS/PIN:** Productness and Value-Bearing Essence, Essential Value Capability, Constitution and Identity Invariants.
- **AIPS/GEAIRM:** qualified intelligence and AI Product boundaries; no assumption that “driver” means an AI Product.

These references describe authority scopes; their exact release versions and cross-specification adoption manifests are still subject to INFOPS release governance. No upstream text is amended by this teaching page.
