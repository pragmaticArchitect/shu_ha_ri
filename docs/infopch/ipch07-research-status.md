## Material unresolved source tensions

**`REU-R02 = OPEN`: narrow/regulatory/time-bounded Product scope.** DPCH07 includes a special consideration for narrow, regulatory and time-bounded Products, potentially including one legitimate regulatory consumer, while its canonical/general requirement demands actual independent adoption. D21/D22 do not authorize an automatic exception to `REU-M03`; this tension is reserved for competent Second-Pass research and owner disposition.

**`REU-R08 = OPEN`: producer-assisted adoption.** DPCH07 and AIPCH08 differ in their treatment of producer coordination and substantial consumer-specific customization. Incidental documentation/onboarding help must be distinguished from indispensable bespoke Product reconstruction, without prematurely resolving borderline `PARTIAL` versus `NOT_MET` cases.

## Mandatory criteria and evidence treatment

`REU-M01–REU-M07` are **conjunctive** for `MET`; there is no individual numerical weighting or averaging. `REU-N01–REU-N08` are negative conditions requiring substantiated counter-evidence; `REU-ES01–REU-ES07` support claim-specific Product/Version attribution. Full maturity requires **both structural reuse design and actual independent adoption**, plus stable semantic/consumption contracts and producer-independent repeatable consumption. Invocation volume, shared technical assets and hypothetical additional use cases do not independently demonstrate reuse of an Information Product.

D02 eligibility, applicability, `EVIDENCE_LIMITED` and `NOT_ASSESSABLE` remain independent. D03 `MET=1`, `PARTIAL=0.5`, `NOT_MET=0` is experimental **characteristic-level** scoring only, without criterion averaging, caps or IPCH L0/L1/L2 maturity bands. Product/Version, semantic-definition/Version, realization revision, compilation and runtime occurrence evidence must remain distinct.
