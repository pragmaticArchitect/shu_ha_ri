## Mandatory and independent compliance boundaries

The eight `CBD-M01–CBD-M08` criteria are **conjunctive for `MET`**. An applicable constraint must be made visible at design time under competent federated governance authority, bound to the exact Product/Version, represented as machine-interpretable executable control, and actually enforced across applicable runtime and consumption paths with attributable detection and evidence. A policy document, DPP trust statement, human sign-off or runtime 'allow' signal alone does not establish full compliance.

**Federation:** DPCH09 describes Global → Divisional → Domain → Product; AIPCH10 describes Global → Domain → Product. These differences remain explicitly preserved (`CBD-R01 OPEN`); IPCH09 mandates no single enterprise organizational hierarchy. Competent policy/legal authority determines applicability, precedence and authorized Product-local conditions; the assessor cannot issue waivers.

**Human oversight:** Legitimate human authorization or review can form part of an enforced control mechanism. Unstructured manual approval does not replace otherwise required computational enforcement. Boundary `CBD-R02 OPEN`.

**Derived information:** A derived or aggregated Information Product may have restrictions beyond those of its source Data Products; their scope and enforcement must be established under competent authority. Policy version, Product/Version, semantic version, realization revision, compilation, runtime occurrence and evidence effective time remain separate.

**DPP:** May support a Product-bound evidence chain; neither DPP implementation nor conformance is imposed as an independent universal `CBD` criterion. Policy existence, actual enforcement and substantive legal compliance are distinct determinations. The assessor must avoid circular claims via IPCH06 trust or IPCH08 monitoring.
