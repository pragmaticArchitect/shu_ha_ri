## DPP and trust-record authority (unresolved)

DPCH06 defines DPP as the **canonical carrier for Data Product trust signals**. AIPCH07 permits **AIPS-DPP or an equivalent governed trust record**. Both remain faithfully identified, without silently equating their implementation or conformance rules. IPCH06 requires objective, current, attributable and machine-consumable Product-level trust evidence exposed through a governed Product-bound trust record. **INFOPS-specific DPP equivalence and conformance authority remain NOT_ESTABLISHED; TRU-R03 is OPEN.** Neither existence of a DPP nor an alternative record alone proves `MET`; independently competent assurance must substantiate material claims.

## Mandatory criteria and evidence treatment

`TRU-M01–TRU-M07` are **conjunctive** for `MET`, without criterion averaging. `TRU-N01–TRU-N08` are independently substantiated failure indicators; `TRU-ES01–TRU-ES07` support claim-specific, Product/Version-attributable evidence. Evidence must be current and appropriate to informational constitution, intended risk and context. Material incidents, restrictions, assurance limitations, semantic/temporal and provenance issues must be faithfully represented; trust records do not create the competence of issuing authorities.

D02 eligibility, applicability, `EVIDENCE_LIMITED` and `NOT_ASSESSABLE` remain separate. D03 `MET=1`, `PARTIAL=0.5`, `NOT_MET=0` is an **experimental characteristic-level** mapping, without criterion averaging, caps or IPCH L0/L1/L2 bands. Product/Version, semantic-definition/Version, realization revision, compilation, runtime occurrence and evidence as-of/determination times are not interchangeable.
