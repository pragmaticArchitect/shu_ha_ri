## Assessment and evidence boundaries

- Mandatory, conjunctive: `DEC-M01–DEC-M06`. Enterprises may choose implementation technologies and assurance stringency, but may not dilute these claims and retain equivalent IPCH03 conformance.
- Negative/counter-evidence conditions: `DEC-N01–DEC-N07`.
- Evidence requirements: `DEC-ES01–DEC-ES06`; no universal format, engine or compiler required.
- Conceptual adversarial fixtures: `DEC-A01–DEC-A12` — **UNEXECUTED**.
- Outstanding research: `DEC-R01–DEC-R06` — **OPEN** for Second Pass.
- D02 maturity findings: MET, PARTIAL, NOT_MET, NOT_APPLICABLE; `EVIDENCE_LIMITED` is an evidence qualifier and `NOT_ASSESSABLE` concerns eligibility. D03 experimental scoring is MET=1, PARTIAL=0.5, NOT_MET=0, with equal characteristic weighting; no criterion averaging, L0/L1/L2 bands or automatic caps.
