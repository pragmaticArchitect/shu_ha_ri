## Governing economic-accountability distinctions

**Costs.** Attribute material build, run, support, shared platform and upstream expenditure as far as defensible for the exact Product/Version and observation period. Estimates and allocation methods must disclose limitations; no universal chargeback or funding-model mandate is introduced.

**Consumption.** Product usage and adoption are observable economic signals, **not proof of realized value**. Multiple consumers and higher usage do not automatically establish higher economic contribution.

**Contextual fitness and value.** Evaluate information against intended decisions and outcomes with appropriately qualified observations. **Correlation is not demonstrated causal contribution.** AIPCH15's `UNKNOWN/LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH` confidence values and COVP artifacts are source examples, not mandatory INFOPS implementations.

**Governed optimization.** Economic choices can justifiably increase cost to protect critical information quality, timeliness, safety or regulation. The Product maturity assessor does not acquire authority to decide funding, monetization, financial allocation, policy exceptions or Product qualification.

**D02/D03.** `ECO-M01–ECO-M08` are mandatory and conjunctive for `MET`. Qualitative `MET`, `PARTIAL`, `NOT_MET`; independent `EVIDENCE_LIMITED`; eligibility-dependent `NOT_ASSESSABLE`; no generic whole-characteristic `NOT_APPLICABLE`. D03 experimental characteristic mapping remains `1 / 0.5 / 0`, with no criterion averaging, automatic caps or IPCH L0–L2 bands.
