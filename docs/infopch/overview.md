# Information Product Characteristics (INFOPCH)

The `INFOPCH01–N` characteristic catalogue is **not yet defined**. P02 will establish the catalogue based on qualified Information Product semantics.

After normative authoring decisions, use the repository template `templates/infopch-characteristic.md`.

Proposed coverage areas for evaluation (non-normative): semantic integrity, contextual interpretability, analytical reproducibility, temporal correctness, Information lineage/provenance, fitness and uncertainty, and general Product maturity.

Characteristics do not independently qualify an Information Product.
