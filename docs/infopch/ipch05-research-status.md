## Mandatory criteria and evidence integrity

`SDC-M01–SDC-M07` require authoritative informational meaning, interpretable output semantics, provenance and dependencies, usage context and limitations, Product-bound structured self-description, independent consumer interpretation, and Product/Version descriptive integrity. All seven are conjunctive for `MET`; none is individually weighted or averaged. `SDC-N01–SDC-N08` remain negative/counter-evidence conditions. `SDC-ES01–SDC-ES07` remain claim-specific evidence requirements. `SDC-A01–SDC-A12` are **UNEXECUTED**; `SDC-R01–SDC-R07` are **OPEN**.

**AIPCH06-aligned DPP treatment:** INFOPROD, an applicable Digital Product Passport (DPP), and/or authoritative Product-bound metadata mechanisms may expose relevant descriptive, semantic, provenance, trust and constraint information. A DPP may be evidence, but is **not** a mandated implementation, an eighth criterion, a DPP conformance prerequisite or automatic proof of `MET`. No `SDC-R08` has been adopted. Independent DPP authority remains separate.

D02 eligibility/applicability and evidence qualifiers remain independent. D03 experimental `MET=1`, `PARTIAL=0.5`, `NOT_MET=0` operates only at characteristic level; `EVIDENCE_LIMITED` is an independent qualifier, not a score. No automatic maturity caps, criterion averaging or IPCH L0/L1/L2 thresholds are adopted.
