## Constitutive composition and authority distinctions

**Direct consumption** uses an already governed Product; **federated/orchestrated consumption** combines existing Products in a workflow without automatically constituting a new Product; **Productized Composition** claims a distinct new Product and requires independent Productness, family/Compound qualification, ownership and governing informational Promise. Routers, orchestrators and dashboards do not become Products merely by assembly. AIPCH20's AI-family AQE/APT requirements are not imposed as Information-family authority.

**Safe combination** requires machine-understandable governed ports and contracts, semantic/analytical/temporal compatibility, dependency and provenance currentness, applicable policy and permitted-purpose continuity, and independence from undocumented implementation internals. Source Product obligations must survive combination. IPCH11 semantic alignment alone does not establish IPCH16 composition safety.

**Evidence** must distinguish assessed Product/Version, semantic-definition version, port-contract revision, dependency versions, composition configuration, realization revision, compilation, runtime occurrence, evidence identity and decision validity. A design or contract is not automatically evidence of executed composition. Derived-Product requirements under IOC-M07 are conditional on an actual composition claim; IOC-M07's Product-boundary responsibility remains mandatory.

**D02/D03** preserve `MET`, `PARTIAL`, `NOT_MET` with independent `EVIDENCE_LIMITED`, eligibility-dependent `NOT_ASSESSABLE` and no generic `NOT_APPLICABLE`. D03 characteristic-level scoring remains 1 / 0.5 / 0; no criterion averaging, automatic caps or IPCH L0–L2 bands.
