## Mandatory non-substitutable observability dimensions

**Operational and informational runtime observability.** Explicit, governed SLA/SLO expectations and automated, Product-level observations at justified cadences, covering relevant behaviour, freshness, quality, dependencies, material degradation, breach detection and actionable routing.

**Recurring automated enterprise PMDD maturity observability.** Executed, automatically recurring maturity assessments of applicable Productization Maturity & Design Dimensions, with Product/Version-attributable snapshots, evaluative criteria versions and historic regressions. This is not implied by dashboards, draft assessment criteria, scheduled plans or approved but unexecuted E0 designs.

All eight `OBS-M01–OBS-M08` are **conjunctive** for `MET`. No criterion-level averaging, new automatic caps or IPCH L0/L1/L2 bands are adopted. Mechanisms and evidence assurance stringency are enterprise-configurable without diluting mandatory claims.

## Material open source and execution tensions

- **`OBS-R02 = OPEN`:** DPCH08's `PARTIAL` case for SLAs without automated PMDD maturity observation versus its stricter simplified `NOT_MET` rule
- **`OBS-R03 = OPEN`:** AIPCH09's dashboard-without-SLO `PARTIAL` case versus its mandatory explicit measurable objectives
- **`OBS-R05 = OPEN`:** What automated enterprise PMDD coverage suffices before all sixteen characteristics have executed or attained normative readiness
- **`OBS-R01–OBS-R08 = OPEN`** in full; no research question has been closed or independently resolved
- **`OBS-A01–OBS-A12 = PROPOSED_E0_UNEXECUTED`**; illustrative fixtures are not actual tests or determinations

**Anti-circularity:** PMDD engine operation alone cannot prove IPCH08 `MET`; each other mandatory Product-level criterion needs independently adequate evidence. The evaluator cannot create underlying Product, semantic, policy, monitoring or assurance authority by consuming evidence.

**Evidence identity:** Keep assessed Product/Version, semantic-definition/Version, realization revision, compilation, runtime occurrence, evaluator/characteristic criteria Version, observation/effective time and evidence source distinguishable. Different historic evaluation versions are not directly comparable unless their comparison basis is justified.

D02 assessment eligibility and applicability are independent of the D03 experimental characteristic-level `MET=1`, `PARTIAL=0.5`, `NOT_MET=0` mapping. `EVIDENCE_LIMITED` remains an independent qualifier, not an automatic negative finding. Approved E0 research designs are **NOT EXECUTED** and do not establish actual recurring PMDD capability.
