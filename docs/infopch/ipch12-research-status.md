## Mandatory intent and qualification boundaries

**Consumer purpose must shape the Product.** Declare governed consumer archetypes and decisions, intended outcomes, an intent-shaped informational Promise, consumer-dependent design/service choices, consumption-led evolution and Product/Version-bound traceability. All eight `INT-M01–INT-M08` are conjunctive for `MET`; do not average individual criteria.

**Single-consumer maturity remains unresolved.** DPCH12 rates a clearly defined single consumer `PARTIAL`; AIPCH13 rates a single clear use case `MET`. `INT-R01` remains OPEN. IPCH12 intentionality is not IPCH07 demonstrated reuse; do not import cross-consumer adoption as an implicit IPCH12 requirement or adopt a universal one-consumer exception.

**Additional unresolved boundaries.** `INT-R02` covers coherent multiple-consumer intents, `INT-R05` covers early-lifecycle feedback and outcome evidence, and `INT-R06` covers legitimate technical/producer-driven change. A feedback *mechanism* is not evidence of *observed feedback-driven evolution*; neither is technical change automatically an intent failure.

**Evidence and authority integrity.** Keep Product/Version, intent definition/revision, semantic-definition/version, consumption contract, realization revision, runtime occurrence, outcome signal and evidence effective times separate. BPS/PIN Productness, Information-family/Compound qualification, GEARA/GEARM, INFOPROD/INFOPDS, DPP, PFI/HDIP, legal/policy and assurance authority are independent. A DPP may be relevant evidence; it is not a separate mandatory conformance criterion. An approved E0 assessment design is not empirical conformity evidence.

**D02/D03 safeguards.** `MET`, `PARTIAL`, `NOT_MET`, independent `EVIDENCE_LIMITED`, competent `NOT_APPLICABLE` and eligibility-dependent `NOT_ASSESSABLE` retain existing meaning. D03 experimental characteristic scores remain `1 / 0.5 / 0`; no criterion-level averaging, caps or IPCH L0/L1/L2 maturity bands.
