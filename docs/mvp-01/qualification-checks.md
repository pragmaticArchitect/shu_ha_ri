# Sixteen approved experimental evidence checks

These checks are **assessment-design questions**, not issued normative conformance requirements. Source authority is independent of this experimental INFOPS profile.

## C1 — Product Constitution (D05)

| Check | Assessment question |
|---|---|
| PCE-01 | Is the exact assessed Product, constituent scope and applicable Version identifiable? |
| PCE-02 | Is there an independently constituted value-bearing governing Product Promise? |
| PCE-03 | Is accountable Product authority identifiable and distinct from hosting/engineering? |
| PCE-04 | Is an independently defensible BPS/PIN Productness determination evidenced? |

Findings: `C1_SUPPORTED`, `C1_NOT_ESTABLISHED`, `C1_SUBJECT_UNRESOLVED`. Even a compelling analytical output cannot self-establish Productness.

## C2 — Analytics Contextual Intent (D03)

| Check | Assessment question |
|---|---|
| ACI-01 | What exact Product Promise governs the assessed subject and Version? |
| ACI-02 | Is analytical Information an intended **primary** value-bearing outcome? |
| ACI-03 | Is the declared Analytics context within applicable GEARA/GEARM responsibilities? |
| ACI-04 | Is Analytics constitutive, rather than consumer usage or enabling computation alone? |

Findings: `C2_SUPPORTED`, `C2_OUTSIDE_SCOPE`, `C2_UNRESOLVED`. **Analytics consumption ≠ Analytics Product constitution.** The Product owner's label alone is insufficient without evidence of the governing Promise.

## C3 — Governed Informational Meaning (D04)

| Check | Assessment question |
|---|---|
| GIM-01 | Is the promised Information Proposition or GEARM-equivalent meaning identifiable? |
| GIM-02 | Are interpretive subject, scope, units, time, assumptions and relevant constraints sufficiently governed? |
| GIM-03 | Is the applicable analytical method, interpretation rationale or meaning-forming basis governed where relevant? |
| GIM-04 | Is the semantic definition, authority, applicable Version and adoption limitation identifiable? |

Findings: `C3_SUPPORTED`, `C3_NOT_ESTABLISHED`, `C3_BOUNDARY_UNRESOLVED`. Semantic constitution is **not** evidence of observed realization correctness. Do not impose a universal numeric formula or mandatory engine on qualitative, evaluative or human-curated analytical Information.

## B0 — Independent Family / Compound Boundary (D05)

| Check | Assessment question |
|---|---|
| FBA-01 | Is the target the entire Product, constituent Product, Version or incidental output? |
| FBA-02 | Are there material independently governed competing value-bearing Promises? |
| FBA-03 | Which independent family and Compound authorities are competent? |
| FBA-04 | Is there traceable compatible, incompatible or unresolved adjudication? |

Findings: `B0_NO_MATERIAL_CONFLICT_IDENTIFIED`, `B0_ADJUDICATED_COMPATIBLE`, `B0_ADJUDICATED_INCOMPATIBLE`, `B0_UNRESOLVED`.

No-material-conflict means only **none was found within examined evidence**, not a proof of universal single-family exclusivity. An incompatible competent determination blocks a provisional profile match and maps to D06's `PROFILE_INCOMPATIBLE`; do not falsely label an adjudicated incompatibility as unresolved.
