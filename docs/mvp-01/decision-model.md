# Overall experimental decision model

`M_0.1(p) = C1(p) AND C2(p) AND C3(p)`, with evidence adequacy and independent B0 boundary procedure constraining any positive decision.

## Six overall outcomes (D02 + prospective D06 addition)

| Result | Meaning | Source mapping |
|---|---|---|
| `PROVISIONAL_INFO_0_1_MATCH` | C1/C2/C3 adequately supported; B0 no material blocking issue | ODR-01 |
| `OUTSIDE_ANALYTICS_SCOPE` | Governing primary Promise affirmatively outside declared Analytics context | ODR-02 |
| `PRODUCTNESS_NOT_ESTABLISHED` | Independent BPS/PIN Productness unavailable or inadequately evidenced | ODR-03 |
| `BOUNDARY_UNRESOLVED` | Material family/Compound/constitutional boundary unresolved | ODR-04 |
| `EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` | Required evidence inadequate for a defensible determination | ODR-05 |
| `PROFILE_INCOMPATIBLE` | Independent competent determination establishes incompatibility for exact subject with bounded profile | ODR-06 |

D02 approved five outcomes. D05 identified the resolved-incompatibility gap. D06 **prospectively** adopted the sixth, without reclassifying historical D02 decisions or parent cases.

## Decision reporting invariants

1. Preserve the exact assessed Product, Version, governing Promise, semantic reference and assessment time.
2. Preserve C1/C2/C3/B0 subfindings, evidence limitations, counterevidence and all material simultaneous blockers.
3. No unqualified `if/else` precedence may silently erase a second applicable deficiency.
4. Document the rationale for selecting a primary overall outcome where multiple findings apply; no universally binding precedence has been adopted (**D08-GAP-01**).
5. `PROFILE_INCOMPATIBLE` requires a traceable **competent** determination and its applicable scope, not merely a rival marketing claim.
6. A draft assessment may leave `profile_result` **unset**; that is an incomplete record, not a seventh overall outcome.
7. A provisional profile match is **not** Product certification, normative qualification or proof of universal Information family membership.

## Minimal research assessment stages (logical, not a prescribed workflow)

Identify subject → collect C1/C2/C3 evidence → assess B0 independently → preserve concurrent findings → apply ODR mapping → record rationale and limitations.

An actual completed Product assessment is **not established** by the existence of these conceptual mappings.
