# INFOPS 0.1 — Analytics-Contextual Information Product Profile

> **Experimental consolidated draft A.1–A.8**, accepted under `INFOPS-0.1-MVP-P01-D08-F01` at conversation level. **Not independently verified or normatively released.**

## A.1 — Applicability and scope

This experimental profile addresses deliberately constituted Products whose **governing primary Promise** is provision of analytical Information in a declared Analytics context addressed by **GEARA** (Analytics architectural responsibilities) and **GEARM** (governing semantic meaning). It does **not** define every Information Product or presume Analytics is universally necessary.

A Data Product used by analysts does not change family merely through that use. A Service, AI, Creative or Compound Product does not become an Information Product merely by producing meaningful or analytical outputs. The *exact constituted subject* matters. An independently constituted downstream Product can have a different Promise and family even when sharing the same representation.

## A.2 — Consolidated qualification model

`M_0.1(p) = C1(p) AND C2(p) AND C3(p)`.

A `PROVISIONAL_INFO_0_1_MATCH` additionally requires adequate claim-specific evidence and B0 screening or a competent compatible determination with **no material unresolved blocker**. C1–C3 are bounded experimental checks, **not** proved universal necessary-and-sufficient family conditions. B0 is procedural, not an intrinsic family predicate.

## A.3 — Minimum qualification checks

| Element | Research-local checks | What is evaluated | Independent authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | PCE-01–04 | Subject/Version; constituted Promise; accountable owner; Productness determination | BPS/PIN |
| C2 | ACI-01–04 | Governing Promise; primary Analytics outcome; Analytics applicability; incidental-use exclusion | Product Promise and GEARA |
| C3 | GIM-01–04 | Information Proposition; interpretive context; meaning-forming basis; semantic definition/Version | GEARM |
| B0 | FBA-01–04 | Whole/constituent boundary; competing Promises; competent jurisdiction; boundary disposition | Family/Compound authorities |

C3 governs **meaning**, not implementation correctness or an engine. Human-curated, manual, external-service, rules-based and AI-assisted realizations may be appropriate depending on the governing Promise.

## A.4 — Profile outcomes

Six owner-approved experimental outcomes: `PROVISIONAL_INFO_0_1_MATCH`, `OUTSIDE_ANALYTICS_SCOPE`, `PRODUCTNESS_NOT_ESTABLISHED`, `BOUNDARY_UNRESOLVED`, `EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT`, `PROFILE_INCOMPATIBLE` (the sixth added **prospectively by D06**, not retroactively to D02). See [decision model](./decision-model.md).

All material concurrent deficiencies must be preserved. An unfinished assessment may have an unset result; this is **not** a seventh outcome.

## A.5 — MPEC-01 minimum evidence contract

Ten conceptual fields: `product_ref`, `product_version`, `bps_productness_ref`, `governing_promise_ref`, `analytics_context_ref`, `information_meaning_ref`, `boundary_assessment_ref`, `profile_result`, `evidence_limitations`, `assessment_provenance`. These are **not** a normative JSON/INFOPROD/INFOPDS schema. Refer to existing independent source authority rather than duplicating it. See [evidence contract](./evidence-contract.md).

## A.6 — Reproducibility

- **R1:** Referential reconstruction of the exact subject, evidence and applicable Versions.
- **R2:** Reasoning reconstruction from evidence to C1/C2/C3/B0 findings and reported ODR outcome.
- **R3:** Independent competent repeatability — future verification objective, **not executed**.

MARC-01–06 articulate reconstruction checks, not passed tests.

## A.7 — Authority and artifact boundaries

- **WHAT:** BPS/PIN Productness, identity, Version, governing Promise, Compound constitution.
- **MEANING:** GEARM semantic Information Proposition, definition and Version; GEARA Analytics applicability.
- **HOW:** Independent realization/engineering, PFI/HDIP and deployment authorities.
- **EVIDENCE / AUTHORIZATION:** Product assurance, conformance, Passport issuance, current eligibility and normative release require separate competent determinations.

Do not conflate Product Kind, instance, Version, semantic definition/Version, realization revision, provider/contract Version, Realization Plan, Blueprint Binding, Compilation Record, execution occurrence, Information occurrence, claim, evidence/provenance, verification, evaluation, accountable decision, historical applicability, latest Version and present currentness.

## A.8 — Non-normative limitations

This profile makes **no claim** to universal ontology, authoritative Product-family exclusion, automatic Compound inheritance, source-pin adoption, correct runtime realization, E1/E2 conformance, Passport authority, or normative release. Competing-family and multi-Promise cases stay with competent authorities. The parent INFOPS scientific programme remains separate and unresolved.
