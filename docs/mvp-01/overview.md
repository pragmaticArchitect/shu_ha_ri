# INFOPS 0.1 — Analytics-contextual MVP research profile

> **EXPERIMENTAL_SPECIFICATION_DRAFT_PREPARED_NOT_VERIFIED** · Owner-approved conversation-level conceptual design (D01–D08, 9 December 2019). **Not** a normative family standard, Product certificate, approved schema, verified candidate, adopted source pin, or authorized release.

## Why a bounded MVP?

The broader INFOPS parent programme continues to investigate whether an intrinsic and universal Information Product ontology can be established. The parallel MVP adopts a deliberately **narrow Analytics context** so a practical experimental 0.1 profile can be documented without claiming that Analytics is necessary for every Information Product.

A representation may be interpreted as Data or Information in different contexts, but **Product-family classification follows the governing constitution and Promise of the assessed Product**, not incidental consumer use.

## Qualification convention

`M_0.1(p) = C1(p) AND C2(p) AND C3(p)`, subject to adequate assessment evidence and independent **B0** family/Compound boundary adjudication.

- **C1 — Product Constitution:** Independent BPS/PIN Productness.
- **C2 — Analytics Contextual Intent:** Primary governing Promise intentionally provides analytical Information in the declared GEARA/GEARM Analytics context.
- **C3 — Governed Informational Meaning:** Applicable GEARM-aligned definition and interpretation.
- **B0 — Boundary procedure:** Independent competing-family/Compound assessment; **not** a fourth intrinsic predicate.

The outcome is at most a **provisional bounded profile match**, never universal Information Product classification.
