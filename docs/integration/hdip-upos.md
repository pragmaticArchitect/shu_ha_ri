# INFOPS within UPOS and HDIP

INFOPS will provide the Information Product family semantics. It should integrate with HDIP's `M14-PP-02` Information / Analytics Product Profile while preserving **GEARM/GEARA** semantic and architectural jurisdiction.

The future HDIP Reference Implementation can illustrate governed discovery for pure consumption **and** intentional Product creation, with optional Concierge or AI Agent mediation in both modes.

Do not impose Data PDEP Stage 0–10, the AI Product lifecycle or any enterprise-specific funding model on all Information Products.
